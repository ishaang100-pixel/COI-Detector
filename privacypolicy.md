## Privacy Policy for Conflict of Interest Detector
Last updated: May 31, 2026
Conflict of Interest Detector does not collect, store, or share any personal data.
When you analyze an article, the text from that webpage is sent to the Anthropic API for analysis. This text is not stored by Conflict of Interest Detector. Please refer to Anthropic's privacy policy at anthropic.com/privacy for information on how they handle API requests.
No user accounts are required. No tracking or analytics are used. No personal information is collected at any time.
